# PKA Prayer Care Context

This glossary defines the prayer-board concepts used across the application.

## Prayer boards

**Main prayer board**:
The shared public board for prayers that are not associated with a cell group.
_Avoid_: global board when referring to the user-facing board.

**Cell group**:
A named community with its own prayer board and shareable slug-based link.
_Avoid_: CG when writing user-facing copy.

**Cell-group prayer**:
A prayer submitted from a cell-group board and visible only on that cell group's board.
_Avoid_: private prayer, unless the prayer is also non-public.

**Prayer origin**:
The board context where a prayer was submitted: either the main prayer board or a specific cell group.
_Avoid_: prayer location.
